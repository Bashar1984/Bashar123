# Directed narration script: "What If AI Suddenly Stopped Working?"

Voice: calm, curious documentary narrator (Jennica). Total about 2:24, 12 scenes of 12 s.
Notation: `[beat]` = short pause (~0.4 s), `[pause]` = full stop pause (~0.8 s), `[long pause]` = ~1.5 s,
`(feeling)` = delivery note, not spoken. **Bold** = stress the word. Tags in square brackets are
ElevenLabs v4 audio-direction friendly, e.g. [whispering], [softly], [warmly].

---

## 01 · THE SWITCH (0:00) — hushed, building tension
(quiet, intrigued, like leaning in to share a secret)
What if **every** AI system on Earth... suddenly stopped? [long pause]
(flat, clipped, a little ominous)
No warning. [beat] No countdown. [pause]
(slow down, drop to almost a whisper on the last word; the clock hits 9:00 on "silent")
Just one moment... when the world's smartest machines went... [beat] [whispering] **silent.**

## 02 · FIRST SIGNS (0:12) — light, a little wry
(casual, everyday, almost amused)
At first, you'd notice the small things. Missing **chatbots**... [beat] broken **recommendations**... [beat] smart tools going offline. [pause]
(shrug in the voice, dry)
Annoying. [beat] **Not** catastrophic.

## 03 · BEHIND THE SCENES (0:24) — serious, rising concern
(lower, steady, "but here's the thing")
But the real disruption would be happening... [beat] **behind the scenes.** [pause]
(informative, ticking off items, even rhythm)
**Banks,** [beat] **hospitals,** [beat] **warehouses,** and transport networks, all use AI for big decisions. [pause]
(heavier, concern)
Some services slow down. [beat] Others need **humans** to take over.

## 04 · THE SLOWDOWN (0:36) — strained, then heavy
(urgent, a bit frantic)
Companies would scramble to restore **manual** workflows. [pause]
(measured, letting the contrast land)
Tasks that took **seconds**... [beat] could take **hours.** [pause]
(slow, tired, deliberate, stretch the last word)
The world wouldn't stop. [beat] It would... **crawl.**

## 05 · WHAT STILL WORKS (0:48) — relieved, grounding
(a breath of relief, warmer)
But most **computers,** [beat] **electricity,** [beat] and the **internet**... would still work. [pause]
(thoughtful, precise)
Only one layer of the stack would go... **dark.** [pause]
(quiet reassurance)
The foundations... are still **standing.**

## 06 · THE TWIST (1:00) — leaning in, a spark of excitement
(brighter, "here's the good part")
Here's the twist. [beat] AI disappearing... wouldn't erase **human knowledge.** [pause]
(confident, proud, two crisp beats)
Doctors could still **diagnose.** [beat] Engineers could still **build.** [pause]
(gentle, matter-of-fact, land on the last line)
We'd lose **speed**... [beat] not intelligence itself.

## 07 · COLLAPSE? (1:12) — question, then calm
(raised, open question; slight tension)
So would civilization... **collapse?** [long pause]
(calm, firm, almost a smile; this is the answer)
**Probably not.** [pause]
(easing, honest, reassuring)
It would be a **rough stretch**... [beat] not the end of the world.

## 08 · HANDED OVER (1:24) — reflective, a little uncomfortable
(slow, quiet, thoughtful; let it sink in)
But the shutdown would reveal something... [beat] **uncomfortable:** [long pause]
(soft, almost to yourself; slow on "handed over")
how much of modern life... we've already **handed over**... [beat] to machines.

## 09 · REDISCOVERY (1:36) — warm, gentle, the mood turns
(soft smile in the voice, nostalgic)
So people did something they'd almost forgotten. [pause]
(simple, tender, one beat each)
They opened a **map.** [beat] They asked a **neighbor.** [beat] They **wrote it down.**

## 10 · CONNECTION (1:48) — glowing, joyful
(warm, brightening)
Cities grew **louder**... [beat] but **kinder.** [pause]
(playful, affectionate)
Radios crackled. [beat] Children learned to **count on their fingers** again. [pause]
(tender, a little moved)
Strangers... became **guides.**

## 11 · DAY SEVEN (2:00) — hopeful, rising to a resolve
(steady, hopeful, a new morning)
On the **seventh day,** the machines woke up. [pause]
(gentle turn, serious again)
But something had changed. [beat] We didn't need them **less.** [long pause]
(warm, certain, slow and full on the last line)
We needed... **ourselves** more.

## 12 · THE END (2:12) — quiet, resolved, final
(very still; slow; a deep breath before; a slight lift on "again")
The day AI stopped... [long pause] [softly] **humanity... started again.**
(hold the last word, then 1 s of silence before the fade)
