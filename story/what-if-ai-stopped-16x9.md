# What If AI Stopped? — "The Day the Machines Went Quiet"

**Format:** 16:9 horizontal widescreen, 1920x1080, six blocks of about 10 seconds each (~60 s total)
**Style:** Editorial mixed-media collage. Archival photo cutouts with thick white paper borders, flat color fields, halftone and paper grain textures, hand-drawn marker annotations, and snappy motion-graphics animation. Not photorealistic and no live action.
**Palette:** burnt red #D83A2E · mustard #F4C542 · charcoal #202020 · ivory paper #F4EBDD · muted cyan #4C9CA8
**Applies to every block:** layered foreground, midground and background spread across the frame; key subjects inside the central 80% of the frame; no vertical stacking; no readable text, letters or numbers. Each block opens on the last frame of the block before it.

---

## Block 1 — 8:59 AM: The World on Autopilot
**Narration:** "Every morning, billions of quiet decisions were made for us. Which road to take. Which word to use. Which patient to see first."

**Visual prompt:** A wide panoramic collage city made of torn ivory and mustard paper buildings under a giant burnt-red sun disc with halftone shading. Thin cyan marker lines flow between buildings, cars, phones and a hospital cutout, linking everything like a glowing nervous system. Archival cutouts of commuters with white borders sit in the foreground. Halftone clouds drift in the background.
**Camera:** A sweeping horizontal FPV glide left to right across the rooftops, following the cyan data lines.
**Transition:** The camera catches up with one cyan line as it speeds toward a clock-shaped circle.

## Block 2 — 9:00 AM: Silence
**Narration:** "Then, at exactly nine o'clock, it stopped."

**Visual prompt:** The cyan lines snap and fall like cut threads, curling into ink strokes on the paper. A long row of collage screens and phones spans the frame, each showing a frozen charcoal loading circle. Traffic-light cutouts go dark. Archival cars stand still at a striped intersection. A charcoal halftone shadow spreads across the scene from right to left.
**Camera:** A lateral tracking shot along the row of frozen screens with a controlled speed ramp. It slows sharply at the moment of the snap.
**Transition:** The camera tracks into the dark center of one loading circle.

## Block 3 — The First Hours: Chaos
**Narration:** "Planes waited on runways. Shelves went unfilled. Nobody knew who was supposed to know."

**Visual prompt:** A widescreen montage in three layers. The foreground has crowd cutouts in white paper borders with abstract red marker squiggle bursts around their heads. The midground holds an airport runway of torn charcoal strips with paper planes standing still, and a supermarket shelf with empty mustard boxes. The background has jittering burnt-red diagonal stripes like an alarm. Halftone dots pulse in rhythm.
**Camera:** A fast FPV weave through the layers with quick snap speed ramps and a slight handheld paper jitter.
**Transition:** A torn piece of paper flies into the lens and fills the frame.

## Block 4 — Rediscovery
**Narration:** "So people did something they had almost forgotten. They opened a map. They asked a neighbor. They wrote it down."

**Visual prompt:** The torn paper unfolds into a huge vintage paper map that stretches across the whole frame. Archival hands cutouts trace routes with red marker. Around the map are a doctor's stethoscope cutout, a stack of library books, a notebook with hand-drawn ink diagrams (no readable writing), and two neighbors leaning over a fence. The light turns warmer, with mustard halftone glow spreading across ivory paper.
**Camera:** A slow cinematic push-in from a wide view of the map toward the hands drawing a route.
**Transition:** The red marker route grows off the map and leads into the next scene.

## Block 5 — Human Connection
**Narration:** "Cities grew louder, but kinder. Radios crackled. Children learned to count on their fingers again. Strangers became guides."

**Visual prompt:** A collage town square laid out horizontally. A teacher's chalkboard covered in abstract chalk shapes, an old radio cutout sending out cyan sound rings, kids in white-bordered cutouts holding up fingers, and a crowd sharing tea at a long table. Strings of mustard paper lights hang overhead. A burnt-red sun sets behind torn paper rooftops.
**Camera:** A smooth 180° orbit around the town square, with layers moving at different speeds for parallax.
**Transition:** As the orbit finishes, the sun disc lines up with the center of the frame.

## Block 6 — Day Seven: The Return
**Narration:** "On the seventh day, the machines woke up. But something had changed. We didn't need them less — we needed ourselves more."

**Visual prompt:** Cyan data lines reappear, softer and thinner. This time they weave around the people and stay out of their hands. People's cutouts stand in the foreground holding both a paper map and a phone. The collage city from Block 1 returns, now covered in hand-drawn marker annotations, notes and human doodles layered over it. A burnt-red sun rises at the center, ringed with halftone.
**Camera:** A slow, wide pull-back that reveals the whole city as one big collage spread on an ivory paper desk. It closes on a held, centered composition.
**End frame:** The full collage at rest with a gentle paper grain flicker, then a cut to ivory paper.

---

**Closing line (narration):** "The day AI stopped, humanity started again."

**Camera rhythm:** FPV glide → lateral track with speed ramp → FPV weave → push-in → orbit → pull-back.
